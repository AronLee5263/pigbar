# PIGBAR website concept

Static Korean/English restaurant website concept for PIGBAR in Hapjeong. The publishable site lives in `dist/`. Run `node preview.mjs`, then open `http://127.0.0.1:4173/`.

## Content sources

- [Naver Place](https://naver.me/5XJIY3Cf): current menu (last listed update 2026-09-15), prices, address, hours, contact, service description and Korean visitor review themes. Checked 2026-09-29.
- [Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Ffdk7cAi9mKsqfgm6): destination link and English review source.
- [Wanderlog Google review excerpts](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/7828983/pigbar--hongdae-korean-bbq-restaurant--%E7%83%A4%E8%82%89--%E3%82%B5%E3%83%A0%E3%82%AE%E3%83%A7%E3%83%97%E3%82%B5%E3%83%AB--%E7%B4%94%E8%B1%86%E8%85%90): five-star review authors and dates. The site paraphrases their comments.

## Media

The four PNG files in `dist/assets/` are AI-generated concept food images, not photographs of the restaurant's actual dishes. Replace them with photos supplied by PIGBAR before an official launch. The middle video links to [Mallesh Baithi's Pexels clip](https://www.pexels.com/video/tasty-korean-bbq-pork-belly-grill-closeup-37253004/) and uses a local poster if the remote video is unavailable. Replace it with a PIGBAR clip when available.

The site lists 16 items from Naver's online menu (last updated September 15, 2026) and 24 additional sets, sides, drinks and add-ons found in older Naver menu-board photos. The older items are visibly marked as needing an availability and price check.

Menu and hours may change. Check Naver Place before publishing. The one-time October 6–7 closure notice hides automatically after October 7, 2026 (KST).
