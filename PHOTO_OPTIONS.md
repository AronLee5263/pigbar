# Pork-neck photo selection

## Current banner order

1. Existing charcoal-grill representative photo.
2. Ten temporary alternatives labeled 01 through 10, in that order.
3. Existing beef doenjang stew, fried rice and draft beer photos.

There are 14 slides. The global counter reads 2 / 14 for candidate 01 and 11 / 14 for candidate 10. Use the candidate label when choosing a photo. Swipe or use the previous/next buttons; the carousel does not advance automatically.

The first pork-neck signature card still uses pork-neck-grilled.jpg. A final replacement will be made after the user chooses an alternative. The original pork-neck.jpg and approved sharing logo are retained.

## Sources and files

The alternatives were selected from the visible Naver Place pork-neck photo category:
https://pcmap.place.naver.com/restaurant/1006983247/photo?filterType=AI%20View&subFilter=MENU_NAME%3A%EB%AA%A9%EC%82%B4

Naver labels this category as AI-selected. Photos show the restaurant's grilled pork in different serving and grilling compositions; category membership is not independent verification of the cut in every image.

PHOTO_OPTIONS.json records each candidate's number, original image URL, Naver source index and dimensions. The locally hosted WebP files are in dist/assets/pork-neck-option-01.webp through pork-neck-option-10.webp. Maximum dimension is 1200px, combined size about 1.84MB, with lazy loading enabled.

All four pages use the same images and numbers. Captions, image alternatives and carousel controls follow the page language.
